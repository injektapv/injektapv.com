How it works

Three steps from solar power to hot water

Injekta is installed inline into the existing heating circuit. No intervention in the domestic hot water tank required.

1

Detect surplus

Your energy management system detects surplus PV electricity and routes it to Injekta instead of feeding it into the grid at low tariffs.

2

Power-to-Heat

Injekta heats the domestic water via a circulation line, entirely outside the hot water tank.

3

Maximize self-consumption

The hot water tank absorbs the heat and stores it thermally until it is consumed by the household.
