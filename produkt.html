The Product

Hydraulic. Inline. No tank intervention.

Injekta is available in three power classes — the right variant for every PV system and household.

Simplex
0.3 to 1.9 kW

The most compact variant for smaller PV systems and 1 to 2-person households. Simple retrofitting, no intervention in the domestic hot water tank.

  • Power range0.3 to 1.9 kW
  • InstallationInline heating circuit
  • Tank interventionNot required
  • EMS compatibilitySystem-agnostic
Duplex
1 to 6 kW

The most recommended variant for standard households. Step-by-step power control for optimal use of PV surplus, fully outside the hot water tank.

  • Power range1 to 6 kW
  • InstallationInline heating circuit
  • Tank interventionNot required
  • EMS compatibilitySystem-agnostic
Triplex
1 to 9 kW

For large PV systems of 10 kWp and above, and households with high hot water demand. Highest power class, inline and without tank intervention.

  • Power range1 to 9 kW
  • InstallationInline heating circuit
  • Tank interventionNot required
  • EMS compatibilitySystem-agnostic
